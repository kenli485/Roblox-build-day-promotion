03 / Good to know

Big ideas.
Small steps.

Who can join?+

Anyone curious about making games! Build Day is designed for young creators, whether you’re opening Studio for the first time or already have a few builds under your belt.

What should I bring?+

A computer with Roblox Studio installed, a Roblox account, and one wild game idea. A grown-up nearby is a great teammate, too.

What happens after?+

Your game is yours to keep improving. We’ll share beginner resources so your first build can become your next big adventure.
