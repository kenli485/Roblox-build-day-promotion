03 / Questions

Good to know.

Who is Build Day for?+

It is designed for young people who are curious about making games, including first-time creators. Educators can share it with families or use it as an optional enrichment activity.

Does our school need a Roblox program?+

No. Students need a computer, a Roblox account, and Roblox Studio. The experience is fully online and can be completed from home or school.
